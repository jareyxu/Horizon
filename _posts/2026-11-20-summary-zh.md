<p class="archive-assessment">AI 判断：标题初筛（未依据正文）：这是数据库软件版本更新，属于面向开发者的技术内容；标题信息有限，普通用户没有直接可用的收益。</p>
<details class="archive-original"><summary>展开原始摘录</summary><p>PostgreSQL 19 Released<br>Most years PostgreSQL ships a new release in September, but this year&#x27;s release came two months later<br>thanks mainly<br>to the flurry of issues that the PostgreSQL team needed to address as part of the ongoing AI-reported vulnerability apocalypse.<br>This release was definitely worth the wait.
